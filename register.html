PERSONAL INFORMATION

LEADERSHIP DETAILS

You can register as an ordinary member without claiming a leadership office.

CHAPLAIN DETAILS

The Parish Chaplain must be male and must select the appropriate Roman Catholic Church title. The appointment is subject to verification and does not grant Super Admin access.

APPOINTMENT DETAILS

If you currently serve as a Patron or Matron, you may submit the relevant appointment claim(s). Parish and prayer-house claims are verified separately. These appointments do not grant Super Admin access.

Each parish position has one slot, and each prayer house has one Patron and one Matron slot. Claims are subject to verification.
